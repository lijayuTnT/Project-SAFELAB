Building the Safety Infrastructure
for the Agentic Era

XSafe AI is an AI safety company incubated by the Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI at Fudan University.

We build the infrastructure that helps intelligent systems remain safe, reliable, and governable.

Our work spans safety evaluation, safety data generation, foundation model enhancement, runtime defense, and agent governance.

Why We Exist

01

Intelligence is becoming agentic.

AI systems are moving beyond generating answers. They are beginning to plan, decide, and act.

02

Agents will operate in the real world.

They will use tools, access systems, collaborate with other agents, and interact with physical environments.

03

Safety must evolve with intelligence.

As AI becomes more capable and autonomous, safety must become a foundational capability — not an afterthought.
